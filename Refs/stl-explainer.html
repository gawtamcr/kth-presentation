Preliminaries · four stages

From a requirement to a gradient

How a Signal Temporal Logic formula becomes a number, how that number becomes differentiable, and how the whole construction is built from the formula automatically.

1

STL — a language for requirements

A requirement is written as a formula built from real-valued predicates on the state, Boolean connectives, and two temporal operators restricted to a bounded window .

Syntax
a predicate — some function of the state crosses zero, for example a clearance or a distance to a goal
eventually — the child formula holds at some time in the window
always — the child formula holds at every time in the window
until — the first child holds continuously until the second becomes true
note The Boolean semantics only say yes or no — a trajectory either satisfies the formula or it does not, with nothing in between.
2

Robustness — a graded margin

Robustness replaces the true/false answer with a signed real number. The sign still tells you satisfaction; the magnitude now tells you by how much.

Recursive definition, by structure of the formula
Soundness — the property everything else depends on
why Once satisfaction is a number instead of a bit, a planner can push on it with a gradient. A truth value gives no direction to move in; a margin does.
3

Smoothness — making the margin differentiable

Exact are continuous but not differentiable everywhere, and where they are differentiable, the gradient flows through only one argument at a time — the rest of the trajectory gets no signal. Each is replaced by a smooth surrogate with a sharpness knob .

Log-sum-exp surrogate

Small κ

Gradient spreads across many timesteps. Score is far from exact.

Large κ

Score approaches the exact one. Gradient concentrates on one timestep again.

Cost of smoothing — a bounded bias, not unlimited slack
rule Guide the sampler with the smoothed score (it has a usable gradient). Decide satisfaction with the exact score (it is actually sound).
4

Compilation — formula to tensor graph

The robustness definition is structurally recursive — it recurses one-for-one over the formula's syntax tree. Compilation runs that same recursion as a program, so building the score is mechanical and needs no case-by-case design.
